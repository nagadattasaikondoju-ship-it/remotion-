# One Loop B-roll catalogue (Drive folder 1hD2ac0YRejkVVhFfPBrYrpVGIjxQAKNY)

Checked on 12 Nov 2026. I looked at all 132 files: 110 videos, 4 frames each, and 22 photos. Every file in Drive is now renamed to
`CATEGORY - what it shows - flags - length - MVI_xxxx.MP4`. In Drive's alphabetical sort, the same kinds of shot sit together.
The camera number stays at the end, so `edl.txt` files and older notes that use `MVI_0456` still find their clip.

## Shoots
- **Oct 2026 (0417–0467, IMG_0419–0445).** Green polo with glasses: rooftop terrace and cafe work day, bike POV rides, the Radaar signboard, and dusk walks.
- **Nov 2026 night (0610–0643, IMG_0590–0600).** HITEC City / Knowledge City at night from a moving car: SATTVA, Orbit, T-Works, Cafe Niloufer, the cable bridge and flyover traffic. Then office desk details.
- **Nov 2026 day (0680–0741).** Floral shirt with glasses at the eLaichi cafe. The day runs in this order:
  1. planning in a notebook;
  2. tea;
  3. arriving with a backpack;
  4. laptop work;
  5. coding and the site going live;
  6. a phone call;
  7. typing the **One Loop website** with "audit / build / market / automate / scale" in the search bar;
  8. a meeting with a black-jacket guest (handshake, planning, laughing);
  9. paying the bill;
  10. walking out and riding off.

**People.** Files name people by outfit, not by name: green polo, floral shirt, check shirt, black-jacket guest, dark-shirt guest.
Names are added only after the user confirms who is who (house rule: no name tags without asking).

## Before using, check
- **Phone calls (0713, 0714, 0715, 0716).** A caller name, and in 0714 a phone number, are readable. Blur them.
- **Client and third-party content.**
  - 0451 / 0452 show a client's signboard (Radaar).
  - 0448 shows other creators' faces on a phone.
  - 0708, 0710, 0711, 0712 show a client site.
- **Landscape files.** Clips flagged `landscape` are horizontal. Every other video was shot with the camera sideways; turn it upright with `transpose=2`, as in the network-reel `assemble.sh`. For vertical reels, crop landscape clips or use them as a background.
- **Soft or shaky.** Most Nov night clips were shot from a moving car: lots of blur and dark frames. 0612, 0623, 0629 and 0633 are the sharpest.
