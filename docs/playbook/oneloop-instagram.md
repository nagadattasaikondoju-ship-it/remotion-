## Rules learned
- **Reach comes from the "if I handled your ___" build:**
  - the viewer's niche named in the first frame;
  - an empty-to-finished build on the beat;
  - about 10 s, ending on the finished state so it loops;
  - readable with the sound off;
  - a niche keyword call to action in the caption.
- **"About us" content stalls even in the same style.** The 6-steps reel used the same person, shoot and captions as ep. 02 and got 748 views. The subject must be the viewer's business.
- **Talking heads reach followers only.** Use them as Part 2s, reply reels and DM follow-ups, not for reach.
- **Episodes are concepts.** Label each as a concept in the caption, and check that the mock brand name isn't a real business.
- **Proof follow-ups** use only the verified case studies in `oneloop-brand.md`.
