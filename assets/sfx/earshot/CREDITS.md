# Earshot sound bank renders
Rendered with `earshot sfx render` (earshot, MIT, github.com/Leonxlnx/earshot @ 591fa3e).
The `fit/*` sounds are fitted from CC0 recordings: earshot's bank of real-recording-based sounds, not pure synthesis.
- land/fingertip_book.wav: fit/land.light/fingertip_book (seed 1)
- land/finger_wood.wav: fit/land.light/finger_wood (seed 1)
- tick/domino.wav, tick/domino_b.wav: fit/tick/domino (seeds 1, 2)
