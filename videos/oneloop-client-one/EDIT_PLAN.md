**The editing style this edit uses (the "edit grammar"):**

- **Voice-first and face-led.** Founder content builds trust on the face, so cuts happen on a change of thought, never on a timer.
- **B-roll goes in the middle stretch (about 12–30 s), where retention sags.** Each insert runs 0.8–1.5 s, and the original voice keeps running underneath.
- **One stylised moment only:** the text match cut, where one word holds still on screen while the pages behind it flash. Here that word is "One Loop".
- **Rejected:** constant zooms, random light flashes, meme cut-ins, and slowing down speech.

- **Lighting runs from day to dusk to night through the story**, which mirrors the story getting darker. I'm keeping that as intentional chapter changes rather than forcing the clips to match.

## 5. Captions (One Loop palette)

- **Palette from your logo:**
  - copper gradient `#E8A273 → #C8784A` for the 1–2 keyword(s) per line;
  - white for the small words;
  - black soft shadow, with no heavy stroke;
  - copper full stops like the logo's "AUDIT**.**".
- **Typography, matching your references:**
  - small words in a light, letter-spaced geometric sans, lowercase;
  - the keyword big, in an italic serif (Instrument Serif / Playfair Italic), 3–4× the size.
  - Example: `our first` (small) / ***client*** (big copper).
- **Placement:**
  - On static tripod shots, the keyword sits **behind the speaker** (subject matte) on the sky, tree or wall above their head, locked to that surface.
  - On moving shots (selfie, bike POV), it sits in the lower-middle safe area in front of the subject.
  - Captions never cover the eyes or mouth, and never go in the right or bottom Instagram UI zones.
- **No captions** on "we did neither" and "so what did we do next?". There the face is the point.

## 6. Sound

- **Voice:**
  - WhatsApp track for R's section, camera audio elsewhere;
  - denoise, high-pass, de-ess, a light compressor;
  - **loudness-matched across all 4 sources**, mastered to −14 LUFS with a true peak of −1 dB.
- **SFX:** taken from the B-roll's own location sound where it exists (real footsteps, real keyboard, real laptop lid, real phone). I synthesise only what doesn't exist:
  - payment chime (0.35 s);
  - 2 clock ticks;
  - low sub hit on "nothing";
  - micro-hit on "neither";
  - UI power-down;
  - whoosh on the real whip;
  - soft paper flicks under the One Loop cut;
  - a short warm riser into the end.

  All SFX sit 12–18 dB under the voice.

## 7. Missing B-roll (only what the story needs)

1. A **generic "₹1,500 received" UPI notification** on your own phone, recorded or screenshotted. No client name or number visible.
2. **"Site not published"**: a screen recording of a draft site or hosting dashboard showing *Draft / Unpublished*. Any non-client project is fine.
3. *(Optional)* A hand pushing open a shop door, 2 s, for "every door".
4. *(Optional)* A phone face-down on a table, or an unanswered "Calling…" screen.

## 8. Quality checks before delivery

- The speaker changes only at chapter boundaries.
- There are at most 3 punch-ins and 2 match cuts.
- The face stays visible and stable on the 3 eye-contact lines.
- Every B-roll shot proves the words it sits under.
- No real client is identifiable.
- SFX stay at least 12 dB under the voice.
- Lip-sync is frame-accurate.
- The last frame cuts out on "worked with" with eye contact, which makes Part 2 the cliffhanger.
