## PART 3: Editing DNA (reference)

### Pacing
- **Shots:** 6 visual segments in 16 s (≈ 2.7 s each).
- **Graphic events:** about 19 in 16 s, so **one every ≈ 0.85 s.** The visual cut rate is moderate; the *graphic* rate is very high.
- **First 3 s:** 3 events (strike, glitch, top caption), plus the CU at 2.5 s.
- **First 10 s:** 4 shots, about 12 graphic events.
- **Fastest:** 2.5–4.6 s (CU + ring + top caption + chip, all inside 2 s).
- **Slowest:** 11.25–13.0 s (one caption, no props). This is the deliberate "relief" hold.
- **Shape:** escalating clutter (0–11 s), then a clean release, then a re-hook.

### Cuts and transitions

| Time | Type | Before → after | Motivation | Why not a plain cut |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.75 | Glitch (vertical blinds, ≈ 4 frames) | Hook → "You know content matters" | The idea change | A visible event resets attention at 1 s |
| 2.50 | Hard cut, a size jump to a 2nd camera (CU, lower angle) | MS → CU | Dialogue (the pain word) | Scale = emotion; the ring makes it a "spotlight" |
| 4.75 | Hard cut back | CU → MS | End of the pain beat | Return to neutral before the next build |
| 5.25 | Overlay in (REC frame) | MS → MS + viewfinder | Dialogue ("you record") | Changes the frame without changing the shot |
| 11.25 | Overlay out | REC off | The relief line | The cleared frame = relief |
| 13.25 | Zoom punch (≈ 2 frames) | MS → CU flash → MS | The turn to proof | An impact without a new angle |

There are no whip pans, light leaks or dissolves; the "transitions" are graphic events.

### Rhythm
- **Feel:** staccato and dense, but legible because each event has one job.
- **Sync:** cuts and pops sync to **stressed words** ("Content", "Camera", "You record", "plate", "Prospects"), not to a beat.
- **Signatures:**
  1. A three-tier caption (small lead / huge glowing lime keyword / small trail that fills in).
  2. A red strike on the word under attack.
  3. UI props as B-roll (ring, REC frame, error chips, play icon).
  4. Captions jump to the top when the face is big.
- **Timing:** the chip "what to say?" lands exactly on "what"; the second chip is about 3 frames late against "how". The CU at 2.5 s cuts mid-phrase ("being on | camera"), which is slightly early but makes the punch feel urgent.

- **Palette:** charcoal/black (wall), warm skin and hair, brown cap, muted green plants, and red/orange art. The graphic accents are lime (#D4FF3F-ish) and red (#E32B2B).

  - Warm-neutral and low-key.

- **Consistency:** one look throughout.

**Recipe (adapted to the bright main footage; no blur or dim, per your note):**
- **White balance:** neutralise the fluorescent green cast, landing on a slightly warm white.
- **Contrast:** a gentle S-curve and a true black point.
- **Shadows:** keep them clean, not lifted.
- **Highlights:** a soft knee at 88 % so the walls keep texture.
- **Saturation:** +8 % chroma, with skin unpushed.
- **Accent:** lime and red graphics only.
- **Texture:** local contrast at 40 % plus a thresholded unsharp mask for an HD read.
- **Vignette:** 8 % at the edges.
