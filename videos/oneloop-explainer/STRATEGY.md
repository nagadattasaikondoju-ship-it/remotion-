## 1. Research summary

**What One Loop is.**
- A Hyderabad-born *growth intelligence collective*: "a full-loop growth company". Audit, Build, Market and Automate are handled under one roof, "instead of you piecing together a web dev shop, a marketing agency, and an automation vendor separately."
- **Core diagnosis (their words):** "Growth breaks down in the gaps between a business's website, its marketing, its operations, and its brand — usually because each piece is handled by a different vendor with no one owning the outcome."
- **Promise (logo tagline):** AUDIT. BUILD. MARKET. AUTOMATE.
- **Line on the site:** "We are OneLoop, closing the gaps in modern growth. We engineer websites that convert, ads that track, and automations that scale."

4. **Automate Operations.** Lead routing, instant follow-ups, dashboards, self-correcting AI.
5. **Scale & Expand.** Compounding month-over-month optimisation.

## 2. Brand essence (12 points)
1. **Category:** full-loop growth company. It is not an agency, not a dev shop and not a tool.
2. **Enemy:** the gaps. That means vendor silos where nobody owns the outcome.
3. **Promise:** one team owns the whole loop: Audit, Build, Market, Automate.
4. **Proof posture:** "Proof over promises. Zero fluff." Verified data or nothing.
5. **Entry point:** a free growth audit. Diagnose before building.
6. **Engineering DNA:** sub-second sites, verified tracking, custom software, AI pipelines.
7. **Growth DNA:** full-funnel Google, Meta and PMax, SEO/AEO/GEO, and content they shoot themselves.
8. **Operations DNA:** automation that removes busywork, including self-correcting AI.
9. **Reach:** born in Hyderabad, working across Australia, Dubai, New Zealand and the UK.
10. **Scale with depth:** 25+ specialists, "not two people wearing ten hats".
11. **Voice:** confident, plain and numeric; short declaratives with one italic emotional accent word.
12. **Visual soul:** one continuous copper loop on black. Every idea is connected to every other.

## 3. Video strategy
- **Goal:** in under 90 seconds, a cold viewer understands what One Loop is, believes it with verified numbers, and books the free audit.
- **Audience:**
  - SMB owners and marketing leads in AU, NZ, UK, UAE and India who already juggle 2–4 vendors.
  - Secondary: enterprise teams looking for one accountable partner.
- **Concept (Wild):** "The Thread That Closes the Gaps".
  - The hook's full stop leaks out of the sentence.
  - It falls into four vendor tiles and leaks out of the gaps between them.
  - The copper drop becomes a **thread** that stitches the vendors into one loop.
  - The loop twists into the infinity and snaps into the **real traced logo**.
  - The same copper element then becomes, in turn: the audit lens, the CTA button, the growth-path rail, the route around the globe and a 3D ribbon carrying the promise. It lands as the logo on the end card.
  - This follows the product prompt's rule that "a through-line object becomes the logo on the end card".
- **Structure (unconventional):** five acts, a problem told as physics, then the method as one continuous UI journey.
  - I. The Leak
  - II. The Loop
  - III. The Growth Path
  - IV. Proof
  - V. Team & Close
- **Pace (Frantic):** something lands or moves on every half-beat. Moves overlap. Every line still gets ≥0.75 s on screen.
- **Sound-off first:**
  - Every idea is carried by on-screen type.
  - The VO column below is an optional read, timed to the same beats.
- **Rules kept:**
  - Real copy and numbers only.
  - UI is rebuilt as vector layers, never a flat screenshot; a cursor drives the clicks.
  - Readable type ≥28 px.
  - One hero number per frame. Bar ratios match the numbers (11:66, 88:753).
- **Banned and avoided:** particle bursts, glows, lens flares, camera shake, RGB split, crossfades, generic gradients, bouncy easing, template look.
  - Springs are critically damped.
  - Easing is expo.
- **Brand exceptions to cinetic's taste rules:** the copper/cream palette and the Instrument Serif italic come from One Loop's own site and logo.
- **Sound:**
  - 120 BPM: kick on every beat, offbeat hats, a sub line.
  - A hit on every scene change.
  - Four risers, each ending on its transition: two metallic (→12 s, →50 s) and two sharp-bass (→18 s, →66 s).
  - The loudest stack is the 66 s riser and hit.
  - All SFX are synthesised as a fallback; the Ultimate Editors library isn't in this repo yet.

## 5. Three alternative hooks
1. **"4 vendors. 0 owners."**
   - Open on the four tiles already drifting apart, then pose the question: "Who owns your growth?"
2. **"$92 → $19."**
   - Open on RMC's cost-per-lead counter falling, with the line: "Same spend. 79% cheaper leads. Here's the loop behind it."
   - Proof-first; strongest for paid social.
3. **"Your website, your ads, your ops and your brand don't talk to each other."**
   - Four chat bubbles go unanswered, then the thread connects them.
