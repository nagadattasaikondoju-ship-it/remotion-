# One Loop: The Growth Path, Swiss Motion Graphics (30 s, 1080×1920, 24 fps)

Brief (defaults taken, per the user's "no pre-approval loop" rule):
- Style: Swiss Motion Graphics. Topic: One Loop's growth system. Brand: copper `#E59C69` on black.
- 30 s vertical for Reels, no voice-over. A type-led film: it works muted, and the type is the narration.
- Original score in the style's palette (motorik pulse and mallets); foley through the user's pipeline (Openverse → Real SFX → Earshot).
- Facts only from oneloop.in, as recorded in `docs/playbook/oneloop-brand.md`.

Colour: the style's paper/ink logic turned to the brand's dark ground.
- Page `#111111`, paper `#1A1A1A`, ink (cream) `#E1E0CC`.
- Greys `#2A2A2A`, `#3A3A3A`, `#6B6B6B`.
- The one signal colour is copper `#E59C69`, and only the thread carries it.

Type: Archivo (OFL), one family, several weights, flush left, lowercase headlines.

## 1. Three structures

1. **The programme**:
   - Five rules (01 audit … 05 scale) are applied to one artifact: a grid of the four parts of a business.
   - The gaps between them are where growth leaks.
   - One copper thread stitches the gaps shut, then becomes the logo.
2. **A countdown 05 → 01**: giant numerals count down to "audit", the free first step. It's a clear CTA, but it reads backwards against "growth".
3. **A comparison**: four vendors side by side on a split spread, until one element crosses every gutter. Strong, but it spends half the film on the problem.

**Chosen: 1.**
- It is One Loop's own thesis, from their site: "growth breaks down in the gaps between a business's website, its marketing, its operations, and its brand".
- It maps exactly onto the style's strongest ideas: the grid means something, and one exception.

## 2. Logline and arc

A business is four separate columns with gaps between them. One copper thread runs the gaps, step by step, until the whole system is one loop.

- **Setup:** four columns, four gaps: "growth breaks in the gaps".
- **Turn:** the copper thread starts at the first gap and works through audit, build, market and automate.
- **Ending:** the grid scales to five markets, the music stops, the thread leaves the grid, draws the figure-eight and snaps into the One Loop mark. Everything else is cut away.

## 3. Benchmark

- **Learn from:**
  - Müller-Brockmann posters: scale contrast and flush-left discipline.
  - Experimental Jetset identity animations: snaps exactly on the beat, nothing decorative.
- **Don't take:** any poster composition, Helvetica, or circle/fan layouts.
