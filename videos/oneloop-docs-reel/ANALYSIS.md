# Reference analysis: "What I send a client the day they sign"

Source: the user's screen recording (`20261112-1948-26`, 548×970, 30 fps, 17.6 s). It is an Instagram Reel played twice: the speaker/mute icon sits bottom right and the content repeats exactly.

- Everything below was measured from frame differences and audio, not guessed. Times are **reel time** (0 = loop start), at about ±1 frame (33 ms).
- Inferences are labelled.

## 0. What it is
- **Type:** Instagram Reel, 8.4 s seamless loop. Faceless-ish "silent reel" with a talking-head pose but no talking.
- **Audience:** freelancers and social-media managers, small agency owners.
- **Genre:** "behind the scenes of my business" / "show your process" + aesthetic cafe vlog.
- **Budget:** phone on the table, available light, overlays made in Canva/CapCut. The documents carry `[YOUR BUSINESS NAME]` and `[Client Name]` placeholders.
  - **Inference:** they are templates the creator sells, so the reel is a soft product showcase.
- **Goal:** authority ("I'm organised, I'm a real business"), plus saves and shares, plus probably template sales via the caption or a comment keyword.

## 1. Executive summary
- **What happens:** a calm woman sips coffee in a warm cafe while five client documents flash up one per second: Contract, Invoice, Welcome pack, Questionnaire, Brand strategy.
- **Emotional promise:** "this is what a professional, put-together business looks like".
- **Style:** low-angle, warm tungsten cafe, editorial serif type, cream-and-charcoal documents.
- **Editing personality:** one continuous take under hard-cut overlays. It's a slideshow of proof on a mood bed.
- **Strength:** the information is the visual. Each document is instantly legible as "pro", and the 8.4 s loop forces rewatches to read them.
- **Weakness:** nothing happens in the footage, the documents are too small to actually read, and there's no on-screen CTA.
- **Why it works:** a big promise in the first frame (the hook text), paid off five times in five seconds, then it loops before you can scroll.

## 2. Hook (first 3 / 5 / 8.4 s)
- **0–1.93 s.**
  - You see: a low-angle wide of a woman sipping from a ceramic cup under glowing pendant bulbs, with a two-line serif caption, "What I send a client / the day they sign".
  - You hear: the song is already at full level, with no intro.
  - The question it opens: *what* do you send?
  - Kind of hook: informational plus aspirational (the "the day they sign" wording implies they *do* sign).
- **1.93–5 s.** Contract → Invoice → Welcome pack. Each answer arrives every ~1 s, on its own beat. The curiosity is paid off immediately and repeatedly.
- **5–8.4 s.** Questionnaire, Brand strategy, then the hook text again over a jump-cut stir/sip. That re-poses the question just as the loop restarts, so the viewer watches the list a second time.
- **The deal with the viewer:** "In under 10 seconds you'll see my whole onboarding stack." Kept.
- **To improve:** show the first document by 1.0 s, not 1.93 s. Make the hook text bigger and give it more contrast; it currently sits on a busy ceiling.

## 5. Editing grammar
- **Default pattern:** one background take; overlays hard-cut every ~1.0 s (the measured holds are 0.93 / 1.03 / 1.07 / 1.07 / 1.20 s).
- **The break:** two background jump cuts in the last 1.2 s refresh the picture before the loop.
- **No transitions, no animation, no SFX.** The cuts sit on the song's pulse. The song loops at exactly 8.40 s: audio autocorrelation peaks at 8.38–8.40 s, r = 0.81.
- **If the cuts were faster (0.5 s):** unreadable. **If slower:** it would feel like a carousel post.

## 6. Cinematography
- **The camera:** a phone resting on the table, ultra-wide lens.
- **Low angle → ceiling bulbs and the subject's chin become the frame → intimacy and "cafe aesthetic" → viewers feel they're sitting across from her.**
- Subject centred; the documents cover the chest, the face stays visible above them.

## 7. Light and colour
- **Light:** tungsten pendants plus window light, all warm (orange/amber).
- **Grade:** natural, slight warmth, mid contrast.
- **Overlays:** neutral cream (#FBFAF6-ish) and charcoal (#232323) against the warm frame. The palette contrast makes them pop without any drop-shadow tricks.

## 8. Mise-en-scène
- Ceramic cup and latte art, wired earphones and a burgundy knit, a Biscoff wrapper on the plate, a tablet on the table.
- **Signals:** "creative freelancer, works from cafes". The prop story (coffee ritual) supports "my business runs calmly".

## 9. Sound
- **Music bed only:** no voice, no SFX.
- **Level:** steady at about −14 LUFS momentary, with a short break/dip at **7.3–8.0 s** (to about −21 LUFS). It lines up with the hook text returning, so the song "breathes" exactly where the question re-appears.
- **Effect:** the music gives the pace; the documents read as on-beat.

## 10. Graphics
- **Titles and labels:** a tall condensed editorial serif, white, with a very light shadow.
  - Hook at about 70 px per line (at 1080 width), two lines, top third.
  - Labels at about 90 px, sitting just above each document.
- **Documents:** Helvetica/Arial-style sans, uppercase headings, tiny body text (decorative, mostly unreadable), placeholders in [brackets].
- **Layout:** each composition fills about 75 % of the frame width, centred slightly right of centre, top at about 35 % of height.

## 15–16. What works / what weakens it
- **Works:**
  - One promise, five fast payoffs.
  - A perfect loop length (8.4 s = the song loop).
  - Labels name each document, so it's readable even at a glance.
  - Escalating value order.
  - Static footage keeps the eye on the documents.
- **Weakens it:**
  - A 1.9 s wait before the first document.
  - The hook text sits on a busy ceiling.
  - The documents are illegible on a phone.
  - No CTA ("comment TEMPLATE").
  - The footage doesn't relate to the documents at all (she's not working).
