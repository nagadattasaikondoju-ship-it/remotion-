## 0. What it is
- **Type:** an 12.7 s seamless-loop Instagram Reel, "silent expert" format: no voice, text-led checklist.
- **Audience:** owners of local service businesses running Meta lead ads (the example is a fitness studio, "Brightwell Studio").
  - **Inference:** it is aimed at gyms and studios, and the creator is an ads freelancer or agency.
- **Goal:** authority, plus saves ("checklist" content gets saved), plus inbound audit requests.
- **Budget:** a phone on a tripod in a home office, and UI cards built in Figma or Canva.

## 1. Executive summary
- **The idea:** a calm guy types at his laptop (a chess board in the foreground means "strategy").
- **Then:** the picture plunges into a near-black, red-lit grade, and nine audit checks appear one per ~1.1 s. Each has a numbered serif title, a one-line plain-English reason and a realistic Ads Manager–style card as proof.
- **The promise:** "I know exactly where your ad money leaks."
- **Strength:** expert-grade specifics (cost per **booking**, not per lead; reply speed; tracking via the CRM) in a format anyone can follow.
- **Weakness:** at about 1.1 s per step nobody can read the cards, so it relies on rewatches and saves. The hook frame is also visually plain.

- **Authority:** specific metrics.
- **Curiosity:** "is my account making these mistakes?".
- **Zeigarnik effect:** the numbered list pulls viewers to the end.
- **Save-for-later:** too dense to read in one pass.
