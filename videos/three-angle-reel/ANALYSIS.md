## Part 3: Editing DNA (reference)

**Pacing.**
- **Cuts:** the master runs in jump-cut segments of 2.5–3.5 s, which averages about 2.4 s across the reel once the montages are counted.
- **First 3 s:** no picture cut at all. The hook is carried by text, with a new kinetic word every ~0.3–0.5 s.
- **Fastest section:** the values montage at 36.6–39.0, a new element every 0.25–0.5 s, with the guest name-drops at 17.9–20.9 (~0.6 s per shot) close behind.
- **Slowest section:** 23.0–36.0, steady talking with captions only.
- **Shape:** wave-like. Calm, a burst (cutaways), calm, a burst (montage), then the ending.

**Cuts and transitions.**

| Time (rec) | Type | Before → after | Motivation | Why not a plain cut |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| approx 2.9, 5.6, 8.7, 16.0, 25.5, 28.0, 31.5, 34.0 | Jump cut | same shot, breath removed | Dialogue (breaths) | It is a plain cut, by design: invisible because the captions keep moving |
| 17.9 | Hard cut to cutaway | host → guest 1 | Dialogue (the name) | Names need faces |
| 18.5, 19.0, 19.6, 20.2 | Hard cuts + edge light bars | guest → guest | Rhythm | The light bars add energy without a whoosh |
| 20.9 | Hard cut back | guest → host | Dialogue | Returns to the speaker |
| 36.0–36.6 | Push-in → scale-down, frame becomes a card | host → card in black space | The word "values" (emotional shift) | Changes the *world*, not just the shot: the viewer knows a payoff is coming |
| 36.6–39.0 | Fly-through / directional blur between cards | card → card | Each value word | Keeps one continuous camera |
| 39.0 | Hard cut back | montage → host | The word "but" | A turn in the argument = a hard return |

**Rhythm.**
- **Style:** staccato text over minimal picture.
- **Cuts:** sync to words and breaths, not music (there's no music bed in the reel itself).
- **Signatures:**
  1. **Kinetic pairs:** a big word plus a small word.
  2. **Letters rising out of a blur:** each letter staggered ~20 ms.
  3. **Grey upcoming words** inside quotes.
  4. **Frame-becomes-card montage.**
- **Timing note:** the cut back on "but" at 39.0 is perfectly placed, since the argument turns on that word.

## Part 6: Typography, graphics and motion design (reference)

**Typography.**
- **Captions:** a geometric grotesk close to Geist / SF Pro Semibold.
  - White, lowercase as spoken (except emphasis caps such as "BEST"), ~5 % of frame height.
  - A soft drop shadow with no box. They sit at chest height, centred.
- **Animation:** letters rise out of a blur with a ~20 ms stagger, and each word appears when spoken. Chunks swap with a hard replace.
- **Kinetic pairs:**
  - big words (~3× caption size, extra-bold, tight tracking) top-left or at chest;
  - small companion words right-aligned or above.

**Motion graphics.**
- **Values montage:** editorial cards (mono annotations, line art, polaroid, triangle diagram, a boxed label "work ethic"), 3D-tilted, flown through with motion blur.
- **Guest names:** typed letter by letter.
- **Unity:** one sans, one mono, one accent colour.
- **Safe zones:** captions stay in the middle band, clear of the Instagram UI top and bottom.

**Style guide we used** (Geist, Geist Mono, Instrument Serif italic accent; white + near-black + mint; red only for cuts and negatives):

| Element | Spec |
|---|---|
| Caption | Geist 640, 64 px at 1080 (128 px at 4K), white, lowercase as spoken, shadow `0 2 3 /30 %` + `0 6 26 /42 %` |
| Caption placement | Chest (y 1480) when the frame is clear; top (y 235, above his hair) when a UI card is up |
| Caption animation | Per letter: blur 9→0 px, rise 14 px, 0.2 s expo-out, 18 ms stagger, each word on its spoken time |
| Kinetic pair | Big Geist 800 150–236 px (−4.5 % tracking) plus small Geist 640 62 px; emotional words in Instrument Serif italic |
| Highlights | Mint for the idea (creative, proof, offer, audience, budget, winner, META); red for the negative (targeting, weakest) |
| UI cards | Paper #F6F5F1 or near-black #121211, radius 40–44, shadow `0 24 60 /28 %`, Geist UI type, Geist Mono labels |
| Motion | Closed-form springs, ζ 0.82 (a hair of overshoot at most); blur follows speed; content swaps with their own enter/exit so text never overlaps |
| Dimming | A gradient scrim behind type only (top or bottom), fading with use. Never blur |

### 3. Hook alternatives (first 1–3 s)

1. **As built:**
   - visual: him to camera;
   - spoken: "Guys, watch this first";
   - text: **watch** / *first.*;
   - sound: whoosh into the split.
   - Works because it's a command plus a promise of a payoff.
2. **Split-first cold open:**
   - visual: open on her "₹15,000" line in split screen;
   - spoken: hers;
   - text: **₹15,000** gone;
   - sound: her voice, no intro.
   - Works because money lost stops the scroll faster than a greeting.
3. **Claim-first:**
   - visual: him full screen;
   - spoken: "Meta ignores your targeting.";
   - text: **targeting** struck red;
   - sound: a strike whoosh.
   - Works because it's a contrarian claim for anyone running ads.

### 5. Style rules
- **Grade:** warm-neutral, soft highlight knee, no blur, no background dimming.
- **Text:** Geist captions; pairs for emphasis; serif italic for emotion words only.
- **Transitions:** hard cuts, plus the two plate moves; no glitches or flashes.
- **Music:** none.
- **SFX:** real recordings, few, on events.
- **Camera:** punch-ins only.
- **B-roll:** only her clip.
- **Captions:** lowercase as spoken, mint and red highlights only.

### 6. Editor instructions (as executed)
- **Pauses:** cut every pause longer than 0.24 s where the audio really drops 24 dB below speech, keeping 60 ms after the last word and 50 ms before the next.
- **Punch-ins:** alternate 1.00→1.025 and 1.09→1.11 on the face anchor (540, 760), Lanczos, one resample straight to 4K.
- **Split:** her square cropped to x 55–865, y 135–855 and scaled ×1.33. The host's half is his own silent tail, ping-ponged, pushed down 650 px. Both panels slide over 0.38 s (expo-out) in and 0.40 s (cubic in-out) out.
- **Plates:** shrink over 0.42 s expo-out to s 0.46 / −3° (scene 1) and s 0.40 / +2.5° (scene 2), with the corner radius growing to 44 px. Return over 0.40 s on cubic in-out.
- **Voice:** DeepFilterNet3 on the whole take before cutting; 12–20 ms fades on every audio cut.
- **Master:** −14 LUFS integrated, true peak ≤ −1.5 dBTP.
