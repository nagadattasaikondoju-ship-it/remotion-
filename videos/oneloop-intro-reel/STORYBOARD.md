# One Loop intro reel — "The Node" (25 s · 1080×1920 · 60 fps · 120 BPM)

## What I found on the live site (oneloop.in, captured 7 Nov 2026 → `site/`)
- **Look:** black ground (#000, cards #161616 with a 1px 5% white border, 16px radius), cream text #E1E0CC, cream button #DEDBC8, copper accent #E59C69. One sans, Almarai (plus an Instrument Serif italic accent and a mono for labels, which this reel drops to keep to one typeface).
- **Hero:** "ONELOOP*" in huge light caps; the pill "● AUSTRALIA · DUBAI · HYDERABAD · NZ · UK"; the cream "Audit my business" button with its round arrow head.
- **The Growth Path:** a copper S-curve with ring nodes, cards alternating left and right: 01 Audit & Forensics (Diagnostic) · 02 Build & Deploy (Engineering) · 03 Market & Track (9x ROAS) · 04 Automate Operations (Zero Waste) · 05 Scale & Expand (Compounding).
- **Built & Deployed by OneLoop:** XP Academy (Live web app, EdTech & Gamification) · Nirvaha (Google Play Store, Mental Wellness & Mobile) · LN Sports Arena (Live website, Elite Sports Infrastructure) · Sithiyam Travel (Live portal, Bespoke Luxury Travel) · Campus Cartel · DocOnCommit.
- **Proven Results. Zero Fluff.:** client tabs that turn into a copper pill when active; four count-up tiles per client. Every number in the reel is copied from these tabs:
  - RMC Plumbing Services (AU): 6.1x lead growth (11/mo → 66/mo) · −79% cost per lead ($92 → $19) · 1,327 total leads
  - Bamboo Spa (NZ): 2.1x conversion growth (1,237/mo → 2,587/mo) · −46% cost per conversion ($1.44 → $0.78) · 40,624 total conversions
  - Bikers Gear UK: 4.6x blended ROAS · £123.9K 10-month revenue (on £26,723 ad spend)
  - Bikers Wear Australia: ~9x return on ad spend, sustained across 17 months · $753K 17-month revenue on $88K ad spend
- **Logo:** the traced vector from `videos/oneloop-showreel/logo.js` (two copper loops, outlined X, ONELOOP wordmark, tagline).

## The through-line object: **the Node**
The copper dot that sits in every Growth Path node (and in the location pill, the card corners and the active client tab). It's the site's own "you are here" mark. In each scene it turns into the next UI element: a full stop that won't stay put → the button's arrow head → a card's corner dot → the timeline node the cursor drags → a product's LIVE light → the active client tab → the "x" of 9x → the pen that draws the logo's two loops.
Copper is used only on the Node and on each scene's one key word.
