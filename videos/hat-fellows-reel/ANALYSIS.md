- **MAIN video:** `VID_20261103_175222` (65 s, 1080×1920, 60 fps). A founder-voice talking head recommending HAT Fellows.

**Creative strategy summary (≤150 words).**
- The reference works because it's a calm, credible talking head, interrupted on schedule by bright paper cards that turn one idea into one big word.
- The main video has the same raw ingredients: a sincere peer voice, a question hook and a selective offer. It lacks that rhythm, and its look is flat.
- The strategy is to keep the face for trust and cut every pause, then let HAT's own design system (cream paper, ink, lime) play the reference's "paper card" role at the four beats that sell: the name, the rooms, the launch and the selection.
- Captions follow the reference's two-tier grammar: a small lead line, then a big keyword, with the CTA words boxed in lime.
- The music is a light lo-fi bed under the face only. Each card is carried by a whoosh, a pop or a stamp.
- The CTA is compressed into one clear instruction ("Comment LINK") and repeated on an end card with the URL.

## PART 3: Editing DNA (reference)

### Pacing
- **Overall:** 12 visual segments in about 34 s (talking-head blocks + cards). That's an **average segment of about 2.8 s**, but a **caption change every 0.9–1.3 s**. The visual cut rate is moderate; the *text* cut rate is high.
- **First 3 s:** one shot (the hook stays on the face), with 4 caption changes, i.e. about 0.8 s per text beat.
- **First 10 s:** 3 segments (face → card → face), about 3.3 s each.
- **Middle (0:14–0:28):** the fastest section, alternating face/card/face/card in 1–2 s slices (0:14–0:17.5).
- **Ending (0:28–0:34):** the slowest. It's one held talking-head take for 6 s, with only the captions changing, so the CTA can be heard.
- **Shape:** wave-like. Hold the face, spike with a card, hold again. Cards land on idea boundaries, not on the beat.

### Cuts and transitions

| Time | Type | Before → after | Motivation | Why not something else |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0:04 | Hard cut + brightness smash | Dark face → white paper "growing &" | Dialogue ("So as we are growing") | The contrast itself is the transition. A wipe would soften the jolt. |
| 0:07 | Hard cut | Card → face | End of the number beat | Return to trust quickly. |
| 0:14 / 0:15 / 0:16 | Hard cuts at phrase ends, voice continuous (L-cut feel) | Card ↔ face ↔ card | Dialogue phrasing | It keeps the sentence unbroken while the picture changes. Quick, but nothing cuts mid-word. |
| 0:16 | Hard cut + sub drop | Face → icon card | Sound (sub drop) + confession | The low hit gives the admission weight. |
| 0:18–0:25 | No cut (continuous take) | — | — | The captions provide the motion, and the long hold reads as honesty. |
| 0:26 | Hard cut | Face → "simple way" card | Payoff word | The payoff earns a fresh frame. |
| 0:28 | Hard cut | Card → face | CTA | Face for the ask: people act on people. |
| Text pops throughout | Scale-pop + 3D box | — | Words / stressed syllables | Gives the eye a beat even when the picture holds. |

There are no whip pans, glitches, light leaks or speed ramps. The style is disciplined.

### Rhythm
- **Visual rhythm:** minimal, flowing. Cuts sync to **phrases and stressed words**, not to the music.
- **Signatures:**
  1. A two-tier caption (small lead + big keyword).
  2. A red box on the one word that matters.
  3. A white paper card for the "headline" words.
  4. A clay-style 3D icon on a confession.
- **Timing:** the 0:16 card lands exactly on "scripting" (perfect). The 0:14 "content creators" card is slightly **late**: it overlaps the next phrase's lead-in, so the face returns mid-thought.

## PART 4: Cinematography and visual language (reference)
- **Lens:** normal-to-short tele (≈ 50–85 mm equiv.), with mild compression and no distortion.
- **Depth of field:** shallow. The background practicals fall into bokeh. Focus is deep enough to hold the face and the mic, with no focus pulls.
- **Camera height and perspective:** eye level, which reads as a peer conversation.
- **Composition:** centred subject, with the head in the top third. The mic is a vertical foreground line, a recurring "podcast" signal. Negative space above the head stays dark.
- **Motifs:**
  - the mic;
  - warm amber practicals;
  - red boxes;
  - white paper;
  - hands framing the keyword.
- **Production value:** the controlled low-key set, practical lights, a matte warm grade, a clean pro mic, wardrobe (a cream cable-knit vest) that pops against the dark ground, and sharp 4K-downscaled detail.
- **Feel:** premium-creator. It's clean and warm, not "studio corporate". The white cards make it feel designed.

## PART 5: Colour, grade and look development (reference)
- **Palette:**
  - Primary: deep brown/charcoal (background).
  - Secondary: cream/ivory (wardrobe, paper cards).
  - Accent: a single saturated red (≈ #D21F2B) for boxes and underlines.
  - Warm amber in the practicals.
- **Temperature:** warm overall (about 4,300 K look). The cards are neutral-cool white, a deliberate contrast.
- **Contrast:** low-key, filmic. Blacks are rich but not crushed (slightly lifted, matte). The highlights on the face roll off softly.
- **Saturation:** natural to slightly muted. Skin is kept honest, and the only loud colour is the red graphics.
- **Skin:** a warm, clean skin line, with no orange push.
- **Texture:** a little softness, possibly a light grain. No halation or chromatic aberration.
- **Consistency:** two looks only (the warm, dark face vs. the white paper), alternated on purpose.

**Recipe (for the face):**
- **White balance:** 150–300 K warmer than neutral.
- **Contrast:** a soft S-curve.
- **Shadows:** slightly lifted, with a hint of warm-brown tint.
- **Highlights:** rolled off with a −0.3 to −0.5 stop knee.
- **Saturation:** −5 % overall, skin protected.
- **Accent:** keep the graphic red the only saturated element.
- **Grain:** 1–2 % fine.
- **Vignette:** 15–20 %.
- **Style:** "warm podcast matte".

## PART 6: Typography, graphics and motion design (reference)
- **Text types:**
  - hook captions;
  - running two-tier captions;
  - card headlines;
  - a script-italic accent ("rewrite");
  - stat ("10K");
  - CTA words (follow, reel, caption below);
  - no lower thirds;
  - no logo.
- **Fonts:**
  - A geometric grotesk (Gilroy/Montserrat-like). The lead line is Regular/Medium, about 30–35 % of the keyword size; keywords are ExtraBold in sentence case.
  - A calligraphic script for single accent words.
- **Colour and treatment:**
  - White text over the face, with a soft shadow.
  - On the cards, black + red.
  - The red boxes have a slight bevel/drop shadow (a faux-3D lift), and "10K" is extruded.
- **Animation:**
  - Lead words fade/slide up word by word.
  - Keywords **scale-pop** with a small overshoot.
  - Underlines **wipe** left→right.
  - The 3D icon drops in with a soft bounce.
- **Timing:** on the spoken word, never ahead of it by more than about 2 frames.
- **Captions:** phrase by phrase, two lines max, centred, sitting on the chest above the mic (≈ 55–60 % of frame height), clear of the face and the platform UI.
- **Motion graphics:** a faint graph-paper grid behind numbers, and clay 3D icons.
- **Consistency:** very high. One sans, one script, one accent colour.

**Style guide (as adapted for HAT, built in this edit):**

| Element | Spec |
|---|---|
| Lead line | Manrope SemiBold, 46 px, white, shadow 0 2 12 rgba(0,0,0,.55), fades up 14 px over 5 frames |
| Keyword | Space Grotesk Bold, 96 px (auto-fit to 980 px), tracking −2.5, white; pops at 0.8 → 1.04 → 1.0 over 6 frames on the spoken word |
| Keyword, boxed (CTAs and payoffs) | Ink #111418 on lime #C9FF52, 10 px radius, **hard 7×8 px ink offset shadow** (HAT's site card style in place of the reference's red 3D box) |
| Strike-through | A lime bar wiping across "meeting people" on "people" |

| Safe zone | Captions centred at y 1235–1450 px (of 1920). Nothing below 1650 px or in the right 120 px (the Reels UI). |
| Shade | A gradient behind the captions (0 → 55 % black from 50 % down), so white text reads over the bright shirt and walls |

## PART 7: Sound design and music (reference)
- **Music:**
  - A lo-fi hip-hop/funk bed (about 85–95 BPM feel; the beat grid is weak because the music sits low).
  - It switches section at about reel 0:00 (a funk vocal-chop section under the hook) and 0:05 (upbeat corporate pop under "10K"). Then it settles into a lo-fi groove.
  - Level about −20 LUFS integrated for the whole reel, with the music well under the voice.
- **Cuts vs beat:** not beat-synced. Cuts follow phrases.
- **Voice:**
  - Warm, mid-pace (≈ 160 wpm), smiling delivery.
  - Stress on the keywords (exactly the boxed words).
  - Short breaths are kept, so it sounds natural.
- **SFX:**
  - A sub-bass drop on the confession card (reel ≈ 0:17.7).
  - Chat-message pops on the boxed words (≈ 0:29.6).
  - A vocal-chop accent (≈ 0:09.6).
  - Short stop-downs (0.3 s near-silence at ≈ 0:00.0 and ≈ 0:03).
- **Mix hierarchy:** voice → SFX accents → music → (no ambience).
- **Addictive trick:** the music changes texture at each card, so the ear gets a pattern interrupt at the same moment as the eye.

**Audio direction sheet (used for HAT):**
- **Music style:** original lo-fi jazz-funk bed (vibraphone 7ths, fingered bass, brushed-feel kit), at 90 BPM with swung 8ths.
- **Placement:** under the face only. It **stops down for every card** (house rule: no music under graphics), so each card arrives as a dynamic drop.
- **Level:** about −19 dB under the voice.
- **Energy curve:** low and constant on the face; spikes come from the risers and hits, not the music.
- **SFX:** 2 risers (into the HAT reveal and into Early Access), each with an impact or whoosh on the cut. Plus:
  - a reverse whoosh into every card;
  - acronym and keyword pops;
  - paper-slap stamps (CXOs / CFOs / CMOs / shortlisted);
  - a marker-brush strike;
  - a notification chime on "live now";
  - a wind-chime glint on the end-card logo.
- **Voiceover:** untouched; voice is king.
- **Mix:** voice on top; SFX −6 to −12 dB relative; music about −19 dB; master −14 LUFS, TP −1.2.

1. **What makes the reference distinctive?** A strict two-look system: a warm, dark podcast face and bright paper cards, glued together by one sans, one red box and phrase-timed captions.
2. **Five techniques to copy exactly:**
   1. The two-tier caption (small lead + big keyword).
   2. Boxing only the action and payoff words.
   3. Hard cuts to full-screen paper cards on headline words.
   4. Keyword pops landing on the stressed syllable.
   5. Keeping the CTA on the face.
3. **Five to adapt, not copy:**
   1. Red → HAT lime with an ink offset shadow.
   2. Script italic → HAT's outlined headline word.
   3. The 3D icon → HAT's own letterpress logo.
   4. The dark set → a background dim/defocus instead of a fake set.
   5. A music bed under everything → under the face only, with stop-downs.
4. **What would hurt HAT:**
   - A red accent, which isn't HAT's colour.
   - Clay emoji icons, which read as "creator template", not a builder fellowship.
   - Fake stats (we used none: "10K"-style numbers aren't in the script).
   - Packing in too many CTAs.
   - A dark moody grade fighting HAT's light cream brand.
5. **The single biggest reason it works:** every second gives the eye one word to read and the ear one human voice to trust, and the cards reset attention right when a sentence lands.

### 4. Pacing map
- **0–3 s:** one shot, 3 caption beats (≈ 1 s each).
- **3–10 s:** 3 shots (≈ 2.3 s), with a caption change every ≈ 1.2 s.
- **10–20 s:** card 1 (3 s) + 4 face shots (≈ 1.5 s): the fastest stretch.
- **20–36 s:** card / face / card / face / card at about 2.5 s each (wave).
- **36–42.6 s:** 4 face shots and the CTA, with longer holds (≈ 1.6–2.2 s) so the ask is heard.
- **42.6–44.6 s:** end card, a 2 s hold.

### 5. Style rules
- **Colour grade:** warm, highlight knee, background dimmed and defocused, vignette 22 %. Never push the cream walls to pure white.
- **Lighting:** don't fake a dark set; keep the face the brightest thing in the frame.
- **Text:** two tiers, max 2 lines, keyword ≤ 980 px wide. Lime box only for the hook payoff, CTAs and the value word ("networking", "AI credits").
- **Transitions:** hard cuts only. A whoosh may only end on a cut.
- **Music:** under the face only, about −19 dB, stopping for cards.
- **SFX:** cards, CTAs and the reveal only. No SFX on ordinary caption changes.
- **Camera movement:** alternate scale between consecutive face shots (1.00 ↔ 1.12–1.20), with a gentle ≤ 6 % push inside a shot.
- **B-roll:** none is available. The cards *are* the B-roll. Real HAT event footage (the site's "Not stock photos" gallery) would be the next upgrade.
- **Captions:** phrase-based and verbatim, with names corrected (Whisper heard "HAT Friends"; the captions say "HAT Fellows").

### 6. Editor instructions (Premiere / Resolve / CapCut / AE)
1. Cut on the EDL in `edl.json`. Remove every pause over 0.3 s and the fillers listed there. Use 12 ms audio fades on every edit so there are no clicks.
2. On the hook, scale to 142 % anchored on the eyes, and keyframe to 150 % over 5.3 s (ease in-out).
3. Alternate the punch-ins at every jump cut: 100 % ↔ 113–115 %. "Drop a comment" gets 120 %.
4. Background: a person matte, then a gaussian blur of about 7 px on the background with −26 % gain. Erode the matte 4 px and feather 11 px so there's no halo around the hair. The tone curve has a highlight knee at 62 %. Use a 22 % vignette.
5. Captions: lead line Manrope 600 / 46 px; keyword Space Grotesk 700 / 96 px. The keyword pops 80 → 104 → 100 % over 6 frames, **on the stressed syllable** (from the word timings).
